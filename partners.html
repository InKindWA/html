Let’s build a more welcoming WA together

We work with community organisations, education providers, councils and businesses.

🏘️

For community organisations

Need volunteers? Ours arrive trained, briefed and supported. They also bring languages, energy and cultural knowledge to your team. We help with one-off event crews and with regular helpers.

  • Train: we prepare volunteers before they start.
  • Place: we match people to your needs.
  • Count: we record the hours they contribute and report them to you.
  • Recognise: we celebrate volunteers, so they keep coming back.
🎓

For universities, colleges and schools

Your international students want connection, and we can provide it. We offer free orientation-style sessions, social programs and structured volunteering. These support student experience, wellbeing and employability.

We can present at orientation, share opportunities through your student services, or run sessions on campus.

Our commitments: every placement is unpaid, with a not-for-profit organisation, and in a role that would not otherwise be paid. Volunteers never replace paid staff.

Support our work

  • organisations that can host volunteers
  • venues for training and events
  • catering, prizes and goods for community days
  • professional skills: design, translation, photography, legal and accounting
  • grants and sponsorship
