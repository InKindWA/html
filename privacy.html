Privacy statement

How we handle the personal information you give us.

What we collect

When you fill in a form on this website we collect the details you enter, such as your name, email, phone number, suburb, languages, interests and availability. We only collect what we need to organise volunteering and respond to you.

How we use it

  • to contact you about volunteering, training and events
  • to match you with volunteering roles and record your volunteer hours
  • to answer your questions and partnership enquiries
  • to report overall, non-identifying numbers (for example, total volunteer hours) to our partners and funders

We do not sell your information or share it with anyone for marketing. We share a volunteer’s name and contact details with a host organisation only when we arrange a placement with them.

Where it is stored

Form submissions are received through our website host, Netlify, which may store data outside Australia, and are sent to our email account. Only authorised InKind WA volunteers and board members can access them.

Photos

We only publish photos of people who have agreed to appear in them. If you change your mind, tell us and we will remove the photo.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or stop receiving emails, at any time. Email admin@inkindwa.org.au.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.
