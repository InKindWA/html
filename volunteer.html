Questions

Does it cost anything?

No. Our training, events and placements are all free.

Can I volunteer on a student visa?

Usually, yes. Volunteering is generally allowed on a student visa when the role is unpaid, is with a not-for-profit organisation, and is not a job that would otherwise be paid. All our placements meet these conditions. Check the current rules for your own visa on the Department of Home Affairs website.

Is my English good enough?

Yes. Many roles need more smiles than words, and volunteering is one of the best ways to practise.

How much time do I need?

As little as one event. There’s no minimum. Study and work come first, and we plan around exam periods.

Will I be paid?

No. Volunteering is unpaid. You gain training, experience, friends and a certificate, and we record every hour you contribute.

I’m not a student or a migrant. Can I join?

Yes, please do! Local volunteers help newcomers feel welcome, and everyone is welcome at our events.

I’m under 18. Can I volunteer?

Please contact us first. Some roles may be possible with a parent or guardian’s consent.
