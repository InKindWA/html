What we do

Train. Place. Count. Recognise.

International students and new migrants already give a lot to WA. We help them give in ways that are seen and valued.

📚

Train

Free, friendly volunteer training, with a certificate when you finish.

🤝

Place

We match you with not-for-profit organisations that need your help.

⏱️

Count

We record every hour you give, so your contribution is seen.

🏅

Recognise

Certificates, references and celebrations for your milestones.

Our program

Pathways to Belonging

A three-year program that helps international students and newcomers Know, Belong and Give Back to Perth. We deliver it together with The Salvation Army Perth City and GoGoPerth. InKind WA leads the volunteer training.

About the program

🧭 Know Perth

Workshops on life in Perth: work rights, healthcare, housing and local culture.

🤝 Belong to Perth

Social events, festivals and gatherings where friendships start.

💛 Give Back to Perth

Free volunteer training and placements with community organisations.

It takes five minutes to sign up. We’ll be in touch within a few days.
