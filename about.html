About us

Two communities, one bridge

We connect international students and new migrants with Western Australia through volunteering.

Our story

InKind WA was founded in 2026 by Perth locals, graduates and community members who kept seeing the same thing. Thousands of international students and new migrants arrive in Western Australia full of energy and goodwill, but find few real ways into the local community.

We believe volunteering is that way in. When a student spends a Saturday at a community festival or an animal shelter, two things happen: the community gains a helper, and the helper gains a community.

InKind WA became an incorporated association in Western Australia in October 2026. We are run by a volunteer board.

Our purpose

To bridge the gap between culturally and linguistically diverse (CaLD) international students, new migrants and the Western Australian community through the transformative power of volunteering.

Our vision

A connected and inclusive Western Australia where international students and new migrants feel they truly belong and actively contribute to a multicultural society.

Our mission

To unite and empower international students and new migrants to understand, engage in and contribute to their local community through meaningful volunteering.

What guides us

Our values

💙 Belonging

Every student and migrant deserves genuine connection and community.

💪 Empowerment

We build confidence, skills and leadership through service.

🌏 Inclusion

We celebrate cultural diversity as a strength of Western Australia.

🤲 Integrity

We act transparently, ethically and in the best interests of those we serve.

🤝 Partnership

We achieve more together, with students, institutions and community organisations.

How we work

Four steps, every year

  1. Educate

    Show students and migrants how volunteering builds skills, wellbeing and career readiness.

  2. Recruit

    Partner with WA institutions and community organisations to match the right people to the right roles.

  3. Train

    Deliver culturally aware training so volunteers are confident, safe and skilled before they start.

  4. Evaluate

    Track hours, satisfaction, skills and community impact, and keep improving.

Governance

InKind WA Inc. is an incorporated association under the Associations Incorporation Act 2015 (WA). We operate under our Rules of Association and a conflict of interest policy, and our board declares and records any conflicts.

Copies of our governing documents are available on request. Email admin@inkindwa.org.au.
